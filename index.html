Short Bio

I am a Postgraduate Researcher and Junior Researcher at Beihang University, specializing in UAV-assisted Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC) systems. I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Civil Aviation University of China in 2024, and I am currently pursuing my M.Eng. in Traffic Information Engineering and Control at Beihang University (expected 2027).

My research focuses on trajectory optimization, interference management, and air traffic management for next-generation autonomous aerial vehicles. At the State Key Laboratory of CNS/ATM, I design hierarchical ISAC systems for urban aerospace applications and develop deep reinforcement learning frameworks for multi-agent UAV coordination.
