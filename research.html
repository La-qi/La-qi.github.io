Research Interests

UAV-Assisted Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC)

My primary research focus is on designing and optimizing UAV-assisted hierarchical ISAC systems for urban aerospace applications. This involves developing novel frameworks for joint sensing and communication in multi-agent UAV systems.

Trajectory Optimization

I work on trajectory planning algorithms for autonomous aerial vehicles, considering constraints such as energy efficiency, interference management, and collision avoidance in dense urban environments.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Systems

Development of DRL frameworks for joint beam control and trajectory design in multi-agent UAV coordination, enabling adaptive and scalable solutions for air traffic management.

Air Traffic Management

Research on next-generation air traffic control systems using machine learning and collaborative reasoning for autonomous air traffic situational awareness and coordination.

Research Methodology

My research combines theoretical optimization, machine learning, and simulation-based validation. Key methodologies include:

  • Mathematical optimization (convex optimization, game theory)
  • Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) algorithms
  • Monte Carlo simulations
  • Real-world system modeling and simulation
  • Multi-agent system coordination
